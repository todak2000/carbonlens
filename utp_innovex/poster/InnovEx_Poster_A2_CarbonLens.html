The Problem
CCUS simulation requires expensive commercial software (USD 50K+/yr) and specialist engineers, locking out most researchers, regulators and early-stage projects.
Our Solution
CarbonLens delivers rigorous CO₂ storage screening and simulation as a free, open-source web application. No install. No cloud fees. Physics runs locally in seconds.

Formation Builder

Set geology, depth, porosity, temperature and trapping type interactively.

Geomechanics Safety

Caprock integrity, fracture pressure and fault risk assessed automatically.

Monte Carlo Analysis

500 realizations in 0.11 s — every output comes with P10/P50/P90 bounds.

Registry Certificate

Sealed, verifiable storage verification records for auditable CCUS projects.
