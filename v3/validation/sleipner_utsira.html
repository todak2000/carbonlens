Physical Interpretation

CO₂ at 10.30 MPa and 37.0 °C is supercritical — above both the critical temperature (31.04 °C) and critical pressure (7.38 MPa). It behaves as a liquid-like dense phase (697 kg/m³) with gas-like mobility, maximising storage density and dissolution trapping.

Density contrast Δρ = 302.5 kg/m³ drives buoyant plume migration. IFT = 28.4 mN/m governs capillary snap-off and residual trapping efficiency. Solubility = 0.8231 mol/kg sets the dissolution trapping capacity.
