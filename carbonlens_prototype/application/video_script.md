# Demo Video Script — 2–3 Minutes
**Prototypes for Humanity 2026**

Upload to YouTube. Include link in application.

---

## Structure

### 0:00–0:20 — The Problem (spoken to camera or voiceover)

"There are 50 countries around the world that have the geology to store carbon dioxide underground — a critical tool for fighting climate change. But the software to evaluate those sites costs $200,000 a year. Most of those 50 countries can't afford it. So they're locked out of the global effort to decarbonise. We built CarbonLens to change that."

---

### 0:20–0:40 — Open the Tool

Screen recording: Open CarbonLens in a browser. Show the impact landing screen.
Voiceover: "CarbonLens runs in any browser. No installation. No license. Free."

---

### 0:40–1:20 — Run a Screening (Niger Delta Formation)

Screen recording:
1. Select Niger Delta Basin preset from the formation panel
2. Show auto-populated geological parameters
3. Click "Run Simulation"
4. Watch the 3D CO₂ plume animation fill the reservoir

Voiceover: "We select the Niger Delta Basin in Nigeria. Real geological data. The tool runs a full multi-physics simulation — pressure, fluid properties, plume behaviour — in seconds. This is the same analysis that takes weeks with enterprise software."

---

### 1:20–1:50 — Show the Results

Screen recording:
1. Geomechanics panel — safety factor, caprock integrity
2. Economics panel — storage cost estimate
3. Executive Summary PDF export — print to screen

Voiceover: "The tool assesses whether the caprock can safely contain the CO₂, estimates the economics, and generates a one-page screening report — ready to share with a minister, an investor, or a regulator."

---

### 1:50–2:10 — Academic Grounding

Screen recording: Methodology panel — showing citations and validation.
Voiceover: "Every equation in CarbonLens is peer-reviewed. The machine learning model was trained on experimental CO₂-brine data from research at Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS in Malaysia. The results are validated against the Sleipner field in Norway — one of the world's longest-running CCS projects."

---

### 2:10–2:30 — Closing

Spoken to camera: "CarbonLens is for the geologist in Lagos who doesn't have a Petrel license. The regulator in Jakarta who needs to evaluate a basin this week, not in six months. The student in Kuala Lumpur doing CCS research on a laptop. Climate science shouldn't have a price tag that locks out the countries that need it most."

---

## Production Notes

- Record screen at 1920×1080
- Use the dark theme for visual impact
- Ensure 3D plume animation is smooth before recording (use Demo Mode)
- Voiceover: calm, clear, not rushed
- Subtitles recommended for international audience
- Total length: aim for 2:30
