01 The Problem
The world must store 6 gigatonnes of CO₂ per year by 2050. Today it stores 50 million.
Under 1% of what net-zero requires (IEA). Suitable formations exist on every inhabited continent. The bottleneck is project development speed: every project starts with a screening question, blocked by two walls.
Enterprise Simulators US$80,000–200,000/year in licences, dedicated workstations, weeks of training. Disproportionate for early screening.
Free Alternatives Require MATLAB or Python expertise and significant setup. Produce no regulatory outputs.
"Projects stall not because the geology is unsuitable: the tools to evaluate it are inaccessible."
