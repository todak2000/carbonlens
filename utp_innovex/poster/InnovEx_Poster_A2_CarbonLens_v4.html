01The Challenge
The world must store 6 gigatonnes of CO2 per year by 2050. Today it stores 50 million.
Under 1% of what net-zero requires (IEA). Suitable formations exist on every inhabited continent. The bottleneck is evaluation velocity: traditional site screening is blocked by two operational friction walls.
Legacy Enterprise Tools US$80,000–200,000/year licences, dedicated hardware, weeks of setup & training. Disproportionate for agile pre-feasibility decision-making.
Complex Scripts & Spreadsheets Require custom programming, fragile setup, and lack standardized compliance outputs or interactive 3D visualizations.
"Project momentum stalls not because the geology is lacking, but because rapid, accessible evaluation tools were absent."
