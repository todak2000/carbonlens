Validation Methodology

CarbonLens validation follows a tiered accuracy framework for screening-level tools:

ModuleAccuracy TargetBenchmark Source
CO₂ Density (Span-Wagner)±0.05% vs NISTNIST Standard Reference Data
CO₂ Density, Impure (PR + Li-Yan)±1-3% depending on impurityPeng-Robinson (1976); Li & Yan (2009)
CO₂ Viscosity (Fenghour)±2% vs publishedFenghour, Wakeham & Vesovic (1998) JPCRD
CO₂ Solubility (Duan-Sun)±5-10% (regression fit)Duan & Sun (2003)
CO₂ Multi-Salt Solubility±8-12% for mixed brinesDuan, Sun, Zhu & Chou (2006)
Plume Radius (Nordbotten)±20-30% vs field monitoringSleipner 4D seismic surveys
Post-Injection Plume (Hesse)Qualitative agreementHesse, Orr & Tchelepi (2008)
Injection Pressure (Theis E₁)±15-25% vs monitored BHPIn Salah, Mt. Simon monitoring
Storage Capacity (DOE P50)Screening-level ±factor of 2DOE/NETL Atlas (2010)
Heterogeneity (Shook/Kopp)±15-25% correction factorShook & Mitchell (2009); Kopp et al. (2010)
Geomechanics (Mohr-Coulomb)Qualitative risk classificationSPE best practices

For the comparison tables in individual formation reports: ✓ PASS = within ±30% of published value; ⚠ REVIEW = within ±50%; ℹ NOTE = outside 50% (expected for screening tools on some metrics).
