Physical Interpretation

CO₂ at 28.00 MPa and 90.0 °C is supercritical — above both the critical temperature (31.04 °C) and critical pressure (7.38 MPa). It behaves as a liquid-like dense phase (679 kg/m³) with gas-like mobility, maximising storage density and dissolution trapping.

Density contrast Δρ = 302.5 kg/m³ drives buoyant plume migration. IFT = 25.0 mN/m governs capillary snap-off and residual trapping efficiency. Solubility = 0.8756 mol/kg sets the dissolution trapping capacity.

About CarbonLens

CarbonLens is a browser-based CO₂ geological storage simulation studio purpose-built for deep saline aquifer CCS screening. It delivers real-time plume simulation, geomechanical risk assessment, and regulatory permit preparation — entirely in the browser.

Developed as MSc research at Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS, Malaysia.
